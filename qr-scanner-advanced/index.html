Privacy Policy – QR Scanner Advanced

Effective date: 11 October 2026

QR Scanner Advanced ("the app", "we", "us") is published by Fintex Global Solutions (Fintex Global Solutions Pvt Ltd). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Your scans, lists, created codes, history and backups stay on your phone. We run no servers that receive them, and we never see them.
  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • The app shows one small banner ad, served by Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID and similar information to show and measure ads, according to your consent choices.
  • You can remove ads with a yearly subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play.
  • We do not sell your information, and the app contains no analytics or tracking tools of our own.

Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhen it is askedWhat it is used for
CameraThe first time you open the scannerScanning codes. Pictures are processed on your phone and are not uploaded.
Location (optional)Only when you tap "Use my location" while creating a location code, or switch on "Location" in a scan list's rulesFilling in your current position. It is saved only with that code or list row, on your phone.

The app never asks for your contacts or photo library permission. When you choose a contact or a picture, Android shows its own picker and gives the app access to only the item you chose.

Information that stays on your device

  • Camera: used only to scan codes. Images are analysed on your phone. A small cropped picture of each scanned code is saved with your history so you can see it later. To make the scanner open faster, the app also keeps one tiny, blurred thumbnail of the last camera view; it is too small to read anything from. None of these pictures leave your phone unless you share them yourself.
  • Scan history, lists, created codes and settings: stored on your phone. You can delete any item, or set the app to delete old history automatically.
  • Contacts (optional): when you pick a contact while creating a code, the app reads only that contact's details (such as name, number and email) to fill in the form.
  • Files you choose: pictures, PDFs and spreadsheets you open in the app, share to it or import are read on your phone only.
  • UPI payment codes: to show the "Pay with" buttons, the app checks which UPI payment apps are installed on your phone. This list is not stored or sent anywhere. When you tap one, the app passes the payment details from the code (UPI ID, name, note and amount) to that app. The payment itself happens entirely inside your UPI app and your bank; the app never sees your bank details or UPI PIN. The app remembers which UPI app you used last, and saves a "payment request" note with that scan, on your phone only.
  • Invoice and Wi-Fi codes: details read from GST invoice, e-way bill or Wi-Fi codes are shown and saved on your phone only. When you choose to join a Wi-Fi network, its name and password are handed to Android to connect.
  • Backups: saved only to the place you choose (for example your phone's storage, or Google Drive through the Android file picker). We do not receive them. The app's data is not included in Android's automatic cloud backup.

Information sent over the internet

The app uses the internet only for these purposes:

  1. Ads (Google AdMob) and consent (Google User Messaging Platform). Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), interactions with ads, and diagnostic information. This is used to show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Google may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox features (such as Topics and Attribution) where available. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before any ads are requested. You can change your choices at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. Learn more about how Google uses information.
  2. Remove-ads subscription (Google Play Billing). Purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's terms. The app receives only a confirmation that your subscription is active. We do not see your payment details.
  3. Link previews (optional, on by default). When you scan a link that the app judges safe, or a known short link, it can visit that web address to show the page title and icon and to reveal where a short link really leads. This sends an ordinary web request to that website, which can see your IP address. Links the app flags as risky are never visited. You can switch this off in Settings → Privacy & safety.
  4. Product and book details (optional, on by default). When you scan a product or book barcode, the app can look up the barcode number on Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) or Open Library (openlibrary.org). Only the barcode number is sent. You can switch this off in Settings → Privacy & safety.

Crash reports, if any, come only from Google Play's standard Android vitals, which Google collects under its own policies when you have allowed it on your phone.

How long information is kept

  • Information on your phone stays until you delete it, use "Delete old history", or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all of the app's data from your phone.
  • We hold no copy of your information, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete.
  • Information collected by Google for ads and payments is kept according to Google's own policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices and rights

  • Switch off link previews and product details in Settings.
  • Change ad consent in Settings → Privacy & ad choices.
  • Reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Google or Privacy settings.
  • Delete items or your whole history at any time, or uninstall the app to remove all its data.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we do not receive or store your information, these requests are normally met by the controls above. For anything else, contact us at the address below.

Security

Everything the app stores is kept in its private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. Connections to Google, Open Food Facts and Open Library use encrypted HTTPS.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
